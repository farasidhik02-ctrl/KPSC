## 1. AIKYA KERALA MOVEMENT — BASIC IDEA

### Q1. What was the Aikya Kerala Movement, and what was its main aim?

**Answer:**

- The **Aikya Kerala Movement** was a movement for the **unification of Malayalam-speaking regions into a single state**.
- During British rule, the present-day Kerala region was politically divided under different administrations.
- Although the people shared a **common culture**, they were governed by different rulers.
- The movement aimed to integrate these Malayalam-speaking regions into **one united Kerala state**.

### Q2. Into which four administrative units was present-day Kerala divided during the British period?

**Answer:**

Present-day Kerala was divided into **four units**:

1. **Travancore** – a princely state.
2. **Kochin** – a princely state.
3. **Malabar** – a district of the **Madras Presidency**, under direct British rule.
4. **Kasargod taluk** – part of the **South Canara district of Mysore State**.

Despite political separation, the people of these regions shared a common culture.

# 2. BEGINNING OF THE AIKYA KERALA MOVEMENT

### Q3. How did the 1920 Nagpur Session of the INC contribute to the beginning of the Aikya Kerala Movement?

**Answer:**

- At its **Nagpur Session in 1920**, the **Indian National Congress (INC)** decided to form **State Congress Committees on a linguistic basis**.
- Following this decision, the **Kerala Pradesh Congress Committee (KPCC)** was formed in **1921**.
- KPCC was created for **all Malayalam-speaking regions**.
- It included people from:
  - Malabar
  - Kochin
  - [Travancore](https://www.keralapscnotes.qd.je/content/history-of-travancore-from-marthanda-varma-to-sree-chithirathirunnal-psc-notes/)

This helped develop the idea that all Malayalam-speaking people should belong to a single state.

### Q4. What role did the KPCC play in developing the idea of Aikya Kerala?

**Answer:**

- From the formation of the **KPCC in 1921**, people increasingly demanded **Aikya Kerala**, a state for all Malayalees.
- A series of **political conferences** were organized under the auspices of KPCC from **1921 onwards**.
- These conferences brought political workers from the three major administrative units onto a **common platform**:
  - Malabar
  - Kochin
  - Travancore
- They encouraged political workers to think in terms of the formation of a **United Kerala State**.

# 3. AIKYA KERALA MOVEMENT BEFORE THE 1940s

### Q5. What happened at the State People's Conference held at Ernakulam in 1928?

**Answer:**

- **State People's Conference** was held at **Ernakulam in 1928**.
- It was attended by delegates from:
  - Malabar
  - Kochin
  - Travancore
- The conference passed a resolution demanding the formation of a **“United Kerala.”**
- The proposed state was to include **all Malayalam-speaking regions**.

### Q6. What was the importance of the 1928 Congress Session at Payyannur?

**Answer:**

- A **Congress Session** was held at **Payyannur in 1928**.
- It was held under the presidentship of **Jawaharlal Nehru**.
- The session passed a resolution asking the national leadership of the Congress to take steps to create **Kerala as a separate province**.
- This was to be done when the **Constitution of Free India** was framed.

### Q7. What was the nature of the Aikya Kerala Movement before the 1940s?

**Answer:**

- During this period, activities for the formation of Kerala were largely restricted to **passing resolutions on different political platforms**.
- However, people involved in the **national movement** generally accepted the idea that there should be a **separate state for Malayalam-speaking people**.

# 4. AIKYA KERALA MOVEMENT DURING THE 1940s

### Q8. Why did the Aikya Kerala Movement gain momentum during the 1940s?

**Answer:**

- During the **1940s**, people began to realize that Indian independence would soon become a reality.
- As independence approached, the movement for the formation of **Aikya Kerala** also gathered momentum.
- The question was no longer merely about passing resolutions; political organizations increasingly discussed concrete plans for creating the new state.

### Q9. What important meeting regarding Aikya Kerala was held in 1945?

**Answer:**

In **1945**, a combined meeting was held involving:

- **Kerala Pradesh Congress Committee (KPCC)**
- **Kochin State People's Congress**
- **Travancore State Congress**

The meeting discussed:

- Plans
- Programmes

to be adopted for the formation of **Kerala as a separate state**.

# 5. SRI KERALA VARMA — “AIKYA KERALA THAMPURAN”

### Q10. Who was known as “Aikya Kerala Thampuran,” and how did he support the movement?

**Answer:**

**Sri Kerala Varma**, the Maharaja of Kochin, was known as:

**“Aikya Kerala Thampuran.”**

In **1946**:

- He sent a message to the **Kochin Legislative Council**.
- He expressed support for the **early formation of a Kerala State**.
- He favoured a state comprising:
  - Travancore
  - Kochin
  - Malabar

He later personally participated in the **Aikya Kerala Conference at Thrissur in 1947** and spoke in favour of a united Kerala.

# 6. AIKYA KERALA CONFERENCE — THRISSUR, 1947

### Q11. What were the major features of the Aikya Kerala Conference held at Thrissur in 1947?

**Answer:**

The **Aikya Kerala Conference** was held at:

- Place: **Thrissur**
- Date: **April 1947**
- President: **K. Kelappan**

Important features:

- Hundreds of delegates from **all parts of Kerala** attended.
- The reigning Maharaja of Kochin, **Sri Kerala Varma**, attended the conference.
- Kerala Varma spoke in favour of establishing a united Kerala comprising:
  - British Malabar
  - Kochin
  - Travancore

### Q12. What did the 1947 Thrissur Aikya Kerala Conference demand?

**Answer:**

The conference demanded the formation of a Kerala State consisting of:

- Princely State of **Travancore**
- Princely State of **Kochin**
- British-occupied **Malayalam-speaking areas**
- **Mahe**

The conference also:

- Passed a resolution moved by Congress leader **E. Moidu Moulavi**.
- The resolution demanded the **early formation of Aikya Kerala**.
- Elected a **Standing Council of 100 members** to follow up the demand through action.

# 7. AIKYA KERALA MOVEMENT AFTER INDEPENDENCE

### Q13. What did K. Kelappan's Petition Committee do after Indian independence?

**Answer:**

- After independence, a **Petition Committee** was formed under the leadership of **K. Kelappan**.
- In **February 1948**, the committee went to **Delhi**.
- It submitted a memorandum to Prime Minister **Jawaharlal Nehru**.
- It requested the **immediate formation of Kerala State**.

# 8. DHAR COMMISSION

### Q14. Why was the Dhar Commission appointed, and what did it do in Kerala?

**Answer:**

- The **Constituent Assembly** appointed a State Language Commission known as the **Dhar Commission**.
- Its purpose was to:
  - Examine problems relating to the creation of new states on a **linguistic basis**.
  - Submit a report to the government.

In **1948**:

- The Dhar Commission visited Kerala.
- It conducted detailed enquiries into the possibility of forming a **Kerala State**.

### Q15. What did the Dhar Commission recommend regarding linguistic states?

**Answer:**

The Dhar Commission:

- **Did not recommend** the formation of states on a linguistic basis.
- Instead, it recommended that states should be formed on the basis of **administrative convenience**.

# 9. ALUVA AIKYA KERALA CONFERENCE — 1949

### Q16. Why was another Aikya Kerala Conference held at Aluva in 1949?

**Answer:**

- Another **Aikya Kerala Conference** was held at **Aluva in February 1949**.
- Its purpose was to persuade the authorities to **hasten the process of state formation**.
- It passed a resolution asking the **Central Government** to create the new Kerala State **without delay**.

However:

- Travancore State Congress leader **Pattom Thanu Pillai** opposed the formation of a **United Kerala State**.

# 10. FORMATION OF THIRU-KOCHI — 1949

### Q17. How and when was Thiru-Kochi formed?

**Answer:**

- Since the Dhar Commission did not support linguistic reorganization, **Travancore and Kochin were merged**.
- A new state called **Thiru-Kochi (Travancore-Cochin)** was formed.
- Date: **1 July 1949**
- It was formed for **administrative purposes**.

### Q18. What happened to the rulers of Travancore and Kochin after the formation of Thiru-Kochi?

**Answer:**

- The **King of Travancore** became the **Raja Pramukh** of the new Thiru-Kochi State.
- The **King of Kochin** was offered the post of **Upa Raja Pramukh**.
- The King of Kochin **refused the post**.
- The source also states that the Maharaja of Kochin **gave up his royal powers unconditionally for the good of the people**.

### Q19. Who headed the first popular ministry of Travancore-Cochin?

**Answer:**

- The first popular ministry of **Travancore-Cochin** was headed by **T.K. Narayana Pillai**.
- He had been the **Chief Minister of Travancore**.
- The ruler of Travancore became the governor of Travancore-Cochin under the title **Rajpramukh**.

# 11. OPPOSITION TO THE THIRU-KOCHI ARRANGEMENT

### Q20. Why did some supporters of Aikya Kerala oppose the formation of Thiru-Kochi?

**Answer:**

Many people opposed:

- Appointment of the **King of Travancore as head of the new state**.
- Formation of a state containing only Travancore and Kochin.

They demanded a larger state comprising:

- **Malabar**
- **Thiru-Kochi**

As a protest against the Central Government's decision:

- **K. Kelappan resigned from the presidentship of the Aikya Kerala Committee**.

# 12. PALAKKAD AIKYA KERALA CONFERENCE — 1949

### Q21. What happened at the Aikya Kerala Conference held at Palakkad in November 1949?

**Answer:**

- Another Aikya Kerala Conference was held at **Palakkad in November 1949**.
- President: **K.P. Kesava Menon**

The conference demanded:

1. Formation of **Aikya Kerala**.
2. Abolition of the post of **Raja Pramukh**.

# 13. POTTI SRIRAMULU AND STATE REORGANIZATION

### Q22. How did Potti Sriramulu's agitation influence the reorganization of Indian states?

**Answer:**

- In **1953**, **Potti Sriramulu** fasted until death.
- His demand was the formation of an **Andhra State for Telugu-speaking people**.
- Following this development, a **States Reorganisation Commission** was established.
- Chairman: **Fazal Ali**.

# 14. FORMATION OF KERALA — 1 NOVEMBER 1956

### Q23. When and on whose recommendations was Kerala State finally formed?

**Answer:**

- Kerala was formed following the recommendations of the **Fazal Ali Commission**.
- Date of formation: **1 November 1956**.

The new state consisted of:

- **Travancore**
- **Kochin**
- **Malabar**
- **Kasargod**

The post of **Raja Pramukh was abolished**.

### Q24. Which Tamil-speaking regions of Travancore were transferred to Madras?

**Answer:**

The Tamil-speaking regions of Travancore were merged with **Madras State**.

These included the southern taluks of:

- **Tovala**
- **Agastheeswaram**
- **Kalkulam**
- **Vilavankod**

Also transferred:

- Eastern part of **Shenkotta Taluk**

### Q25. Which Tamil-speaking region of Malabar was transferred to Madras?

**Answer:**

- The Tamil-speaking region of Malabar was also transferred to **Madras State**.
- **Gudallur** was merged with Madras.

# 15. ROLE OF K. KELAPPAN IN THE AIKYA KERALA MOVEMENT

### Q26. What was K. Kelappan's overall role in the Aikya Kerala Movement?

**Answer:**

K. Kelappan was one of the major leaders of the Aikya Kerala Movement.

- He became **President of the Aikya Kerala Committee formed in 1945**.
- He presided over the **Aikya Kerala Convention at Thrissur**.
- He led the **Petition Committee to Delhi in February 1948**.
- He submitted a memorandum to Prime Minister **Jawaharlal Nehru** requesting the immediate formation of Kerala.
- He later resigned as President of the Aikya Kerala Committee in protest against the **Thiru-Kochi arrangement under a Raja Pramukh**.

### Q27. What territories did K. Kelappan propose for inclusion in Kerala at the Thrissur Aikya Kerala Convention?

**Answer:**

At the Thrissur convention, Kelappan stated that Kerala should be formed with:

- **Kasargod**
- **Coorg**
- **Nilgiris**
- **Malabar**
- **Kochi**
- **Thiruvitamkur (Travancore)**

### Q28. What principles did K. Kelappan say should be considered when creating a separate state?

**Answer:**

Kelappan was the first, according to the notes, to state that state formation should consider not only **language**, but also:

- **Geographical features**
- **Historical background**

### Q29. Why did K. Kelappan resign from the presidentship of the Aikya Kerala Committee?

**Answer:**

- Thiru-Kochi was created with the **Raja Pramukh as its head**.
- Kelappan opposed this arrangement.
- He resigned from the presidentship of the **Aikya Kerala Committee as a protest**.

His objective was a state:

- For **Malayalees**
- Governed by **representatives of the people**

rather than headed through the old princely arrangement.
