### Early Malayalam Press

**Q. Which was the first printed magazine in the Malayalam language?**  
**A. Jnananikshepam**

**Q. In which year was Jnananikshepam, the first printed Malayalam magazine, published?**  
**A. 1821**

**Q. From which place was Jnananikshepam printed in 1821?**  
**A. C.M.S. Press, Kottayam**

**Q. Who were the two persons behind the publication of Jnananikshepam?**  
**A. Arch Deacon Koshy and Rev. George Mathen**

**Q. What were the main purposes of Jnananikshepam?**  
**A. Propagation of religion and dissemination of knowledge** 

**Q. According to the PDF, which was the first newspaper in Malayalam?**  
**A. Rajyasamaharam**

**Q. In which year was the Malayalam newspaper Rajyasamaharam first published?**  
**A. 1847**

**Q. From which place was Rajyasamaharam published in 1847?**  
**A. Tellicherry (Thalassery)**

**Q. Which missionary-scholar was associated with the publication of Rajyasamaharam?**  
**A. Dr. Herman Gundert**

**Q. Which missionary organisation published Rajyasamaharam under Dr. Herman Gundert?**  
**A. Basel Mission**

**Q. What was the main emphasis of Rajyasamaharam?**  
**A. Religion** 

**Q. Which publication was started by Herman Gundert in October 1847?**  
**A. Paschimodayam**

**Q. In which year and month was Paschimodayam started by Herman Gundert?**  
**A. October 1847**

**Q. Who was the formal editor of Paschimodayam?**  
**A. Rev. Fr. Muller**

**Q. Which subjects were covered by Paschimodayam?**  
**A. Geography, history, natural science and astrology**

**Q. Which two publications were started by Herman Gundert in 1847?**  
**A. Rajyasamaharam and Paschimodayam** 

### Beginning of Newspapers

**Q. Which was the first newspaper to be published from Kerala according to the PDF?**  
**A. Western Star**

**Q. In which language was Western Star, the first newspaper published from Kerala, issued?**  
**A. English**

**Q. What was the Malayalam edition of the English newspaper Western Star called?**  
**A. Paschimataraka**

**Q. In which year did the Malayalam edition of Western Star, Paschimataraka, begin publication?**  
**A. 1864**

**Q. From which place did Paschimataraka begin publication in 1864?**  
**A. Cochin** 

**Q. Which two newspapers were started from Kottayam in 1867?**  
**A. Santishtavadi and Travancore Herald**

**Q. Which Malayalam newspaper started from Kottayam in 1867?**  
**A. Santishtavadi**

**Q. Which English newspaper started from Kottayam in 1867?**  
**A. Travancore Herald**

**Q. Which newspaper was ordered to close because it opposed the Travancore Government?**  
**A. Santishtavadi**

**Q. Which newspaper became the first “martyr to the cause of freedom of the press” in Malayalam journalism?**  
**A. Santishtavadi** 

**Q. Which newspaper made its appearance from Cochin in 1870?**  
**A. Keralapataka**

**Q. In which year did Keralapataka make its appearance from Cochin?**  
**A. 1870**

### Keralamitram

**Q. Which publication is described in the PDF as the first “newspaper” in the Malayalam language?**  
**A. Keralamitram**

**Q. Who was the first editor of Keralamitram?**  
**A. Kandathil Varghese Mappilai**

**Q. Which entrepreneur was associated with Keralamitram along with Kandathil Varghese Mappilai?**  
**A. Devji Bhimji**

**Q. Which famous Malayalam newspaper was later founded by Keralamitram's first editor, Kandathil Varghese Mappilai?**  
**A. Malayala Manorama**

**Q. Which Marathi magazine was run from Cochin by Devji Bhimji?**  
**A. Keralakokil** 

# Growth of Press in Malabar

**Q. Which English weekly started publication from Kozhikode in 1879?**  
**A. West Coast Spectator**

**Q. In which year did West Coast Spectator begin publication from Kozhikode?**  
**A. 1879**

**Q. Who printed the West Coast Spectator from Kozhikode?**  
**A. Vakil Poovadan Raman**

**Q. From which press was the West Coast Spectator printed?**  
**A. Spectator Press**

**Q. What was the West Coast Spectator later renamed?**  
**A. Malabar Spectator** 

**Q. Which Malayalam weekly was started from Kozhikode in 1884?**  
**A. Keralapatrika**

**Q. Who conceived the idea of starting Keralapatrika in 1884?**  
**A. Chengulathu Kunhirama Menon**

**Q. From which place was Keralapatrika published?**  
**A. Kozhikode**

**Q. From which press was Keralapatrika printed?**  
**A. Vidyavilasom Press**

**Q. Attendance at which 1884 conference possibly inspired Chengulathu Kunhirama Menon to conceive Keralapatrika?**  
**A. Indian National Association conference at Calcutta**

**Q. Which newspaper did Chengulathu Kunhirama Menon claim was the first Malayalam “newspaper” in Malabar district?**  
**A. Keralapatrika**

**Q. Which ruler subscribed to 200 copies of Keralapatrika for distribution among his officials?**  
**A. Maharaja of Travancore** 

**Q. Which Malayalam periodical was brought out by the Spectator Press of Kozhikode in 1886?**  
**A. Kerala Sanchari**

**Q. Who edited Kerala Sanchari when it was published from Kozhikode?**  
**A. Vengayil Kunhiraman Nayanar**

**Q. What was the pen name of Kerala Sanchari editor Vengayil Kunhiraman Nayanar?**  
**A. Kesari**

**Q. Which Malayalam periodical became known for the sharp humour and witticism of Vengayil Kunhiraman Nayanar?**  
**A. Kerala Sanchari**

**Q. Who was associated with Kerala Sanchari as its editor for some time in 1897?**  
**A. Moorkoth Kumaran**

**Q. With which newspaper did Kerala Sanchari later merge?**  
**A. Mitavadi**

**Q. From which place was Mitavadi, with which Kerala Sanchari later merged, published?**  
**A. Thalassery**

# Samadarshi → Prabhodakan → Kesari

**Q. Who commenced publication of Samadarshi from Thiruvananthapuram in 1918?**  
**A. Kunnath Janardhanamenon**

**Q. In which year did Samadarshi commence publication from Thiruvananthapuram?**  
**A. 1918**

**Q. Who joined Samadarshi as editor in 1923?**  
**A. A. Balakrishna Pillai**

**Q. In which year did A. Balakrishna Pillai become editor of Samadarshi?**  
**A. 1923**

**Q. What did A. Balakrishna Pillai criticise while editing Samadarshi?**  
**A. The corrupt and high-handed bureaucracy of Travancore**

**Q. In which year was A. Balakrishna Pillai forced to resign from Samadarshi?**  
**A. 1926** 

**Q. Which periodical did A. Balakrishna Pillai launch after his association with Samadarshi?**  
**A. Prabhodakan**

**Q. What happened to A. Balakrishna Pillai's Prabhodakan within six months of its appearance?**  
**A. It was banned by the Travancore Government**

**Q. Which periodical did A. Balakrishna Pillai start in 1926 after Prabhodakan was banned?**  
**A. Kesari**

**Q. Who started Kesari in 1926?**  
**A. A. Balakrishna Pillai**

**Q. According to A. Balakrishna Pillai, what purposes should the press serve besides presenting news?**  
**A. Educating the public, disseminating knowledge, encouraging free thought and open discussion**

**Q. Which three publications form the important journalistic sequence associated with A. Balakrishna Pillai in the PDF?**  
**A. Samadarshi → Prabhodakan → Kesari** 

# Mathrubhumi

**Q. In which year was Mathrubhumi started?**  
**A. 1923**

**Q. Who was the editor of Mathrubhumi when it started in 1923?**  
**A. K.P. Kesava Menon**

**Q. In which month and year did Mathrubhumi start issuing as a daily?**  
**A. April 1930**

**Q. During which national movement did Mathrubhumi become a daily in April 1930?**  
**A. Civil Disobedience Movement**

**Q. Whose article criticising the British Army led to government action against Mathrubhumi?**  
**A. Sanjayan**

**Q. What did Sanjayan's article in Mathrubhumi criticise?**  
**A. High-handedness of the British Army**

**Q. Which government banned Mathrubhumi following Sanjayan's article?**  
**A. Madras Government**

**Q. What public reaction followed the Madras Government's ban on Mathrubhumi?**  
**A. A state-wide agitation demanding withdrawal of the ban**

**Q. In which year does the PDF state that Mathrubhumi made a triumphant re-entry?**  
**A. 1947** 

# Al-Ameen & Prabhatham

**Q. In which year did Al-Ameen start publication?**  
**A. 1924**

**Q. Who started the newspaper Al-Ameen in 1924?**  
**A. Mohammed Abdul Rahiman Sahib**

**Q. Mohammed Abdul Rahiman Sahib, founder of Al-Ameen, was a leader of which political organisation?**  
**A. Congress**

**Q. In which year did Al-Ameen begin issuing as a daily?**  
**A. 1930**

**Q. What political position of Al-Ameen led to repeated action by the authorities?**  
**A. Its pro-nationalist stand**

**Q. Which newspaper started by Mohammed Abdul Rahiman Sahib was discontinued on more than one occasion due to action by the authorities?**  
**A. Al-Ameen** 

**Q. From which place did Prabhatham start publication?**  
**A. Shoranur**

**Q. Who was the editor of Prabhatham published from Shoranur?**  
**A. E.M.S. Namboodiripad**

**Q. Prabhatham served as the organ of which political party?**  
**A. Congress Socialist Party**

**Q. Which newspaper had its licence suspended following the publication of a poem on Bhagat Singh's martyrdom?**  
**A. Prabhatham**

**Q. Why was Prabhatham's licence suspended after it published a poem on Bhagat Singh's martyrdom?**  
**A. It refused to furnish security to the government**

**Q. What eventually happened to Prabhatham's suspended licence?**  
**A. It was restored**.
