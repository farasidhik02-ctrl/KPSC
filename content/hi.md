**Answer:** The Portuguese became the **first Europeans to reach India by sea**, marking the beginning of direct European maritime involvement in India.

# 4. Vasco da Gama at Calicut

### 11. How was Vasco da Gama received by the Zamorin of Calicut, and what was the response to the gifts he brought?

**Answer:** Vasco da Gama was received by the **Zamorin of Calicut in the traditional manner and with high respect**, but the **gifts brought by Gama did not impress the Zamorin**.

### 12. How did Vasco da Gama obtain trading rights at Calicut?

**Answer:** Despite the objections of **Arab merchants**, Vasco da Gama managed to obtain a **letter of concession from the Zamorin granting trading rights** to the Portuguese.

### 13. Why did problems arise between Vasco da Gama and the Calicut authorities after he obtained trading rights?

**Answer:** The Portuguese were unable to pay the **prescribed customs duties and the price of their goods in gold**. As security for payment, the Calicut officials temporarily detained some Portuguese agents.

### 14. How did Vasco da Gama react when his Portuguese agents were detained?

**Answer:** Vasco da Gama became annoyed and **forcibly took a few natives and sixteen fishermen with him**.

# 10. Pedro Álvares Cabral

### 32. Who conducted the second Portuguese expedition to India, how many ships did he bring, and when did he reach Kozhikode?

**Answer:** **Pedro Álvares Cabral** conducted the second expedition. He reached **Kozhikode in 1500 with six ships**.

**Answer:** The Portuguese obtained the **right to erect a factory at Calicut**.

### 34. Why was Cabral forced to leave Calicut and sail towards Cochin?

### 35. What were the two major objectives of the Portuguese in Kerala, and who challenged them?

**Answer:** The two major objectives were **trade monopoly and Christianity**. These objectives were challenged by the **Zamorins**.

### 36. How was Cabral received by the Cochin Raja in 1500?

**Answer:** In **1500**, Cabral was **warmly received by the Cochin Raja**, who gave the Portuguese facilities for trade.

### 38. What happened when Cabral went to Cannanore?

### 39. Where did Cabral go after completing his activities in Kerala?

**Answer:** He **returned to Portugal**.

# 14. Almeida and the Joint Fleet

### 49. Whom did Francisco de Almeida defeat in the decisive naval battle?

**Answer:** Almeida defeated a **joint fleet consisting of the Mamluk Burji Sultan of Egypt, the Ottoman Empire, the Zamorin of Calicut and the Sultan of Gujarat**.

### 50. Which four powers were included in the joint fleet defeated by Almeida?

**Answer:**

- **Mamluk Burji Sultan of Egypt**
- **Ottoman Empire**
- **Zamorin of Calicut**
- **Sultan of Gujarat**

### 51. What was the result of Almeida's victory over the joint fleet?

**Answer:** Almeida's victory was **decisive**. The **Ottomans and Egyptians left the Indian Ocean**, which enabled the Portuguese to establish a **trade monopoly over Indian waters**.
