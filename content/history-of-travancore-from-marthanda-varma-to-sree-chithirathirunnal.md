### 1. Who were the rulers of Travancore and what were their periods of rule?

**Answer:**

![](/uploads/History-of-Travancore.webp)

1. **Anizham Thirunal Veerabaala Marthanda Varma** – 1729–1758
2. **Karthika Thirunal Rama Varma (Dharma Raja)** – 1758–1798
3. **Avittom Thirunal Balarama Varma** – 1798–1810
4. **Gowri Lakshmi Bayi** – 1810–1815
  - Ruled pending the birth of a son from **1810–1813**
  - Regent for her newborn son from **1813–1815**
5. **Gowri Parvati Bayi** – 1815–1829
  - Regent for her nephew
6. **Swathi Thirunal Rama Varma** – 1829–1846
  - King from birth in 1813
  - Crowned in 1815
  - Ruled from 1829–1846
7. **Uthram Thirunal Marthanda Varma** – 1846–1860
8. **Ayilyam Thirunal Rama Varma** – 1860–1880
9. **Visakham Thirunal Rama Varma** – 1880–1885
10. **Sree Moolam Thirunal Rama Varma** – 1885–1924
11. **Sethu Lakshmi Bayi** – 1924–1931

- Regent for her nephew

12. **Chithira Thirunal Balarama Varma**

- Born in 1912
- Succeeded in 1924
- Received full power in 1931
- Ruled until 1949
- Titular Maharajah until 1971
- Died in 1991



### 2. What are the important general facts about the Travancore dynasty?

**Answer:**

- **Tripapur Swarupam** was the old name of the Travancore dynasty.
- Travancore kings were known as **Vanchibhupati**.
- **Vanchisha Mangalam** was the anthem of the Kingdom of Travancore.
- The first written code of law in Travancore was **Chattavariolas**.
- **Colonel Munro** prepared the Chattavariolas.
- **Shanku** was the symbol of the Travancore dynasty.
- Chief Ministers of Travancore were known as **Dalava or Dewan**.
- **Nanchinad** was known as the **“Paddy field of Thiruvithamkur.”**
- **Matilakam Granthavali** is associated with the Travancore dynasty.
- **Sri Padmanabhaswamy** was the worship idol of Travancore.
- Travancore kings were known as **Sripadmanabha Dasan**.



### 3. Who held the important “first” positions in Travancore?

**Answer:**

- **First Dalava:** Arumukham Pillai
- **First Diwan:** Raja Kesava Das
- **First British Diwan:** Colonel Munro
- **First Resident Diwan:** Colonel Munro
- **First British Resident:** Colonel Macaulay
- **First Muslim Diwan:** Muhammad Habibullah



# 2. ANIZHAM THIRUNAL MARTHANDA VARMA

## 1729–1758

### 4. Why is Anizham Thirunal Marthanda Varma important in Travancore history?

**Answer:**

- **Anizham Thirunal Marthanda Varma** is regarded as the **founder of modern Travancore**.
- He was born in **1706**.
- He styled himself as the **“Prince of Neyyattinkara.”**
- He defeated the eight feudal landlords known as:
  - **Ettuveettil Pillamar**
  - **Ettara Yogam**
- He renovated **Udayagiri Kotta**.
- He built **Vattakotta**.
- He built **Krishnapuram Palace** in Alappuzha.
- The famous **Gajendramoksham mural** is located in Krishnapuram Palace.
- He merged **Kottarakkara** with Travancore.
- He annexed **Thekkumkur and Vadakkumkur** to Travancore.
- He started the temple ceremony called **Hiranyagarbha**.
- **Kalkulam** was the headquarters of Travancore during his reign.
- **Mavelikkara** was his business headquarters.



### 5. What were the important religious and cultural developments during Marthanda Varma's reign?

**Answer:**

- **Ramayan Dalava** was a famous minister of Marthanda Varma.
- **Murajapam** is an important celebration at the **Sri Padmanabhaswamy Temple**.
- Murajapam was first introduced in **1750**.
- Murajapam is celebrated every **six years**.
- **Bhadradeepam**, conducted twice a year, is associated with Murajapam.
- Marthanda Varma led **Venad towards modernity**.
- Venad became the modern kingdom of **Travancore**.
- The Travancore army became more powerful during his reign.



### 6. What were the important wars, territorial expansions and administrative reforms of Marthanda Varma?

**Answer:**

- The **King of Kayamkulam and Marthanda Varma** signed the **Treaty of Mannar in 1742**.
- **Veera Kerala Varma**, the king of Kottarakkara, died in Marthanda Varma's imprisonment.
- Marthanda Varma captured **Kayamkulam (Odanad)** through the **Purakkad War in 1746**.
- He established a **layered administrative system** in Travancore.
- An individual **Pravritti (village)** was administered by a **Pravarthikar**, a village officer acting on behalf of the state.
- A group of villages formed the **Mandapathum Vathukkal (Thaluk)**.
- It was governed by a **Karyakar**, described in the notes as a prototype of the modern Tahsildar or district revenue officer.
- Marthanda Varma eliminated the feudal barons and established a **centralised monarchy**.



### 7. Which poets and officials were associated with Marthanda Varma?

**Answer:**

- **Ramapurathu Waryar** and **Kunchan Nambiar** were prominent poets who lived in Marthanda Varma's kingdom.
- **Palliyadi Mallan Sankaran** was the revenue minister of Marthanda Varma.
- **Krishna Sharma** was his chief poet.



### 8. What was Thrippadidanam and when did Marthanda Varma perform it?

**Answer:**

- Marthanda Varma performed **Thrippadidanam on 3 January 1750**.
- He dedicated the kingdom to **Lord Sri Padmanabha**.
- He thereafter ruled as the **“Servant of Padmanabha”**, or **Padmanabhadasan**.
- This act is known as **Thrippadidanam**.



### 9. What was the Treaty of Mavelikara of 1753?

**Answer:**

- The **Treaty of Mavelikara** was signed in **1753** between **Marthanda Varma and the Dutch**.
- It was a **peace and friendship treaty** between the [Dutch East India Company](https://www.keralapscnotes.qd.je/content/dutch-in-kerala-arrival-of-europeans-in-kerala-psc-notes/) and Maharaja Anizham Thirunal Marthanda Varma of Travancore.
- It was signed on **15 August 1753**.
- The treaty:
  - Marked the end of Dutch **political and commercial dominance in Kerala**.
  - Established an agreement of **mutual territorial integrity**.
  - Required the Dutch not to interfere in the internal affairs of smaller kingdoms.
  - Required the Dutch not to form alliances with **Travancore's enemies**.



### 10. What was the Battle of Colachel and why was it important?

**Answer:**

- The **Battle of Colachel** was fought between **Travancore and the Dutch**.
- It took place on **10 August 1741**.
- Marthanda Varma defeated the **Dutch**.
- The cause of the war was **disputes over trade**.
- It was the first time an **Asian kingdom defeated a European power**, according to the notes.
- It ended Dutch plans for **colonial expansion in India**.
- The Dutch never regained their former strength and shifted their focus towards **Indonesia**.



### 11. Who was Eustachius De Lannoy and what happened to him after the Battle of Colachel?

**Answer:**

- **Eustachius De Lannoy** was the Dutch commander defeated by Marthanda Varma at the **[Battle of Colachel**.](https://www.keralapscnotes.qd.je/content/dutch-in-kerala-arrival-of-europeans-in-kerala-psc-notes/)
- He later became a **senior admiral in the Travancore army**.
- He trained the Travancore army in **modern warfare**.
- He was known as **“Valiyakappithan.”**
- His tomb is located at **Udayagirikotta**, near **Thakkala in Tamil Nadu**.
- Udayagirikotta was built by **Veera Ravi Varma**.



### 12. What other important facts are associated with Marthanda Varma?

**Answer:**

- **Ammachiplav**, where Marthanda Varma hid, is located near **Neyyattinkara Sri Krishna Swamy Temple**.
- The historical novels **Marthandavarma** and **Dharmaraja** were written by **C. V. Raman Pillai**.



### 13. What were the important wars, treaties and developments during Marthanda Varma's reign?

**Answer:**

- **Treaty of Venad** was signed with the British in **1723**.
- **Attingal** was added to Travancore in **1730**.
- In **1742**, Marthanda Varma captured **Kili Manor**.
- **Treaty of Mannar** was signed in **1742**.
- Under the Treaty of Mannar:
  - The Kayamkulam ruler agreed to merge a major portion of **Kayamkulam with Travancore**.
  - He agreed to pay **1,000 rupees every year**.
- **Kayamkulam was captured in 1746** through the **Battle of Purakkad**.
- During Marthanda Varma's period, the commercial department was known as **Mulakumadishsheela**.
- The industrialists of Travancore were known as **Mulakumadisheelakkar**.
- Marthanda Varma established an army called **Maravapada**.
- **Ponmana Anna** and **Puthan Anna** were dams built during his period.



### 14. Which rulers were associated with the title “Ashoka”?

**Answer:**

- **Marthanda Varma** → “Ashoka of Thiruvithamkur”
- **Vikramaditya Varaguna** → “Ashoka of Kerala”
- **Amoghavarshan** → “Ashoka of South India”
- **Kanishka** → “Second Ashoka”



# 3. KARTHIKA THIRUNAL RAMA VARMA

## DHARMA RAJA, 1758–1798

### 15. What are the important facts about Karthika Thirunal Rama Varma, known as Dharma Raja?

**Answer:**

- **Karthika Thirunal Rama Varma** ruled Travancore from **1758 to 1798**.
- He was known as:
  - **Tripapur Muppan**
  - **Chiravayur Muppan**
  - **Kizhavan Raja**
- He was a contemporary of **Pazassi Raja** and **Shaktan Thampuran**.
- He corresponded with the **Pope at the Vatican**.
- He built **Nedumkota**.
- He was the **longest-ruling ruler in Travancore**.
- He shifted the capital of Travancore from **Padmanabhapuram (Kalkulam) to Thiruvananthapuram**.
- He wrote **Balaramabharata**, a work based on the **Natya Shastra**, also known as **Balarama Bharatam**.
- He was the ruler of Travancore during the invasions of Kerala by **Hyder Ali and Tipu Sultan**.
- He built **Kulashekhara Mandapam** in Padmanabhapuram Palace.



### 16. What was the Treaty of Suchindram?

**Answer:**

- The **Treaty of Suchindram** was signed in **1762**.
- It was an agreement between the native kingdoms of **Cochin and Travancore**.
- The agreement was signed at the **Suchindram Temple**.



### 17. What are the other important facts associated with Dharma Raja?

**Answer:**

- **Colonel Macaulay**, who served from **1795–1810**, was the first British Resident of Travancore.
- **Kunchan Nambiar** and **Unnaivaryar** were prominent poets in the court of Karthika Thirunal.
- In **1766**, Dharma Raja performed the second **Thrippadidanam**.
- **Tipu Sultan attacked Nedungota in 1789**.
- Dharma Raja died in **1798**.
- His major plays were:
  - **Subhadraharana**
  - **Rajasuyam**
  - **Kalyana Saugandhikam**
  - **Panchali Swayamvaram**
  - **Gandharva Vijayam**



# 4. RAJA KESAVA DAS

### 18. Who was Raja Kesava Das and what were his major contributions?

**Answer:**

- **Raja Kesava Das** was the first Prime Minister of Travancore to assume the post of **Dewan**.
- He was known as **“Valia Divanji.”**
- His real name was **Kesavapillai**.
- He received the title **“Raja” from Lord Mornington**.
- Important places built by Raja Kesava Das include:
  - **Alappuzha Town**
  - **Chala Kampolam**
- **Varkala Town** was founded by **Ayyappan Marthandapilla**.
- **MC Road (State Highway 1)** was built by Diwan Rajakesava Das.
- The route mentioned is **Kesavadasapuram–Angamali**.



# 5. AVITTOM THIRUNAL BALARAMA VARMA

## 1798–1810

### 19. What are the important facts about Avittom Thirunal Balarama Varma?

**Answer:**

- **Avittom Thirunal Balarama Varma** ruled from **1798 to 1810**.
- He is described in the notes as the **weakest ruler in the history of Travancore**.
- During his reign, a **trade agreement was signed between Travancore and the British East India Company**.
- **Macaulay** was the first Resident of Travancore.
- **Balaramapuram** and **Vizhinjam Harbour** were built by **Ummini Thampi**, who was the Diwan of Avittom Thirunal.



# 6. VELU THAMPI DALAWA

### 20. Who was Velu Thampi Dalawa?

**Answer:**

- **Velu Thampi Dalawa** was the powerful minister of Avittom Thirunal Balarama Varma.
- **Kalkulam in Kanyakumari** was his birthplace.
- His real name was **Thalakulath Velayudhan Chempakaraman Thampi**.
- He became the **Dewan of Thiruvithankur in 1802**.
- He led the **anti-British struggles in Thiruvithankur**.
- He was assisted by **Paliath Achan**, the minister of Kochi, in the struggle against the British.



### 21. What were the major administrative and economic activities of Velu Thampi Dalawa?

**Answer:**

- He established **Hajoor Kacheri**, the secretariat, in **Kollam**.
- He founded the **Changanassery slave market**.
- He made **Pathiramanal Island** in Vembanad Lake arable.
- He sacrificed his life in **1809**.
- He committed suicide at **Mannadi Temple in Pathanamthitta**.
- His memorial is located at **Mannadi**.



### 22. What was the Kundara Proclamation?

**Answer:**

- **Velu Thampi Dalawa** issued the **Kundara Proclamation**.
- It was issued on **11 January 1809**.
- The corresponding Malayalam calendar date given is **984 Makaram 1**.
- The **Ilampallur Temple** was the temple where the Kundara Proclamation was witnessed.



# 7. UMMINI THAMPI

### 23. What are the important facts about Ummini Thampi?

**Answer:**

- **Ummini Thampi** became the Diwan of Travancore after Velu Thampi Dalawa.
- He built **Vizhinjam Port**.
- He established courts called **Insuwaf Kacherikal** in Travancore.
- He started the **police force in Travancore**.
- Before the police force, there was a force of **Nair soldiers**.
- In **1810**, he founded the **weaving town at Balaramapuram**.



# 8. AYILYAM THIRUNAL GAURI LAKSHMI BHAI

## 1810–1815

### 24. What are the important facts about Ayilyam Thirunal Gauri Lakshmi Bhai?

**Answer:**

- **Ayilyam Thirunal Gauri Lakshmi Bhai** ruled from **1810 to 1815**.
- She was the **first woman ruler of Travancore**.
- She was the ruler who ruled for the **shortest period in Travancore**.
- She abolished the **slave trade in Travancore**.
- Slave trade was abolished on **5 December 1812**.
- She implemented the **secretarial system of administration**.
- She introduced the **Pattayam system**, or land deed system.
- She established **District Courts and Appellate Courts** in Travancore.
- She introduced **vaccination and allopathic treatment**.
- During her reign, **Devaswom administration was taken over by the government**.
- **Swati Thirunal** was the son of Rani Gauri Lakshmi Bhai.



# 9. COLONEL MUNRO

### 25. Who was Colonel Munro and what were his important positions?

**Answer:**

- **Colonel Munro** was the Diwan of Travancore during the reign of Rani Gauri Lakshmi Bhai.
- He became Diwan after **Ummini Thampi**.
- He was the **first European Diwan in Travancore**.
- He was appointed as **Travancore Resident in 1811**.
- He was the **first Resident of Travancore**.
- He was the **first non-Hindu Diwan in the history of Travancore**.
- He prepared the legal code called **Chattavariolas**.
- He served as Diwan in both **Travancore and Kochi**.



# 10. UTHRATTATHI THIRUNAL RANI GAURI PARVATI BHAI

## 1815–1829

### 26. What were the major reforms of Rani Gauri Parvati Bhai?

**Answer:**

- **Rani Gauri Parvati Bhai** ruled from **1815 to 1829**.
- She was the **first regent ruler of Travancore**.
- She abolished **export and import duties**.
- She declared that **education was the duty of the government**.
- She made **primary education compulsory** in Travancore.
- Educational colleges were taken over by the government.
- **Parvati Puthanar** was constructed during her period.
- Parvati Puthanar is a canal connecting:
  - **Veli Backwater**
  - **Kadinamkulam Backwater**
- She allowed lower castes to wear **gold, silver and other jewellery**.
- She issued a proclamation allowing everyone in her kingdom to **tile the roofs of their houses**.
- **Coffee cultivation** was introduced into Travancore for the first time.
- She abolished **Uzhiyam Vela**.
- She was the ruler during the period when the **London Mission Society** began its work in Travancore.
- The London Mission Society was first started at **Nagercoil**.



# 11. SWATI THIRUNAL RAMA VARMA

## 1829–1846

### 27. What are the important titles and cultural achievements of Swati Thirunal?

**Answer:**

- **Swati Thirunal Rama Varma** ruled from **1829 to 1846**.
- He was known as:
  - **Dakshina Bhojan**
  - **Garbhashreeman**
- He was known as the **“King of Musicians”** and **“Musician of Kings.”**
- He was fond of **Carnatic music** and **Veena Vayana**.
- He abolished the ancient punishment method called **Suchindram Kaimuk**.
- He started **English education** in Travancore.
- **Mohiniyattam** flourished during his reign.
- He managed **more than twenty languages**.
- The Travancore army was given the name **Nair Brigade** during his reign.
- His reign is known as the **“Golden Age of Modern Travancore.”**



### 28. What were Swati Thirunal's administrative and educational contributions?

**Answer:**

- He moved **Hajoor Kacheri** from **Kollam to Thiruvananthapuram**.
- He established the **English School in Travancore in 1834**.
- The school later became:
  - **Raja's Free School**
  - **University College**
- Swati Thirunal was the **first king of Kerala to appear on a postage stamp**.
- **Iraimman Thampi** was his head poet.
- **“Omanathingal Kidavo”** was composed by Iraimman Thampi.



### 29. Who were the Thanjavur Nalvar associated with Swati Thirunal?

**Answer:**

- The head scholars of Swati Thirunal's congregation were known as the **Thanjavur Nalvar**.
- The members were:
  - **Vadivelu**
  - **Ponnaiah**
  - **Chinnaiah**
  - **Sivanandan**
- They were collectively known as the **Thanjavur Nalvar Sangam**.



### 30. What were the important “firsts” and institutions associated with Swati Thirunal?

**Answer:**

**First in Travancore:**

- **Census** → Swati Thirunal, **1836**
- **Regular Census** → Ayilyam Thirunal, **1875**

**Institutions established by Swati Thirunal:**

- **Thaikkad Hospital**
- **Horsemanship / Kuthira Malika**
- **Trivandrum Zoo**
- **Observatory**
- **Travancore Public Library**
- **Nakshathra Bungalow**
- **Court of Munsif**

**Departments initiated by Swati Thirunal:**

- **Irrigation Department**
- **Engineering Department**
- **Department of Agriculture**



# 12. UTHRAM THIRUNAL MARTHANDA VARMA

## 1847–1860

### 31. What are the important reforms and events associated with Uthram Thirunal Marthanda Varma?

**Answer:**

- **Uthram Thirunal Marthanda Varma** ruled from **1847 to 1860**.
- He issued a proclamation **freeing the children of slaves**.
- He gave permission to **Channar women to wear upper clothes** on **26 July 1859**.
- He established the **Pen Pallikkoodam** in Thiruvananthapuram.
- He was the King of Travancore during the **1857 Revolt / Sepoy Mutiny**.
- He was the ruler during the establishment of the **post office system in Travancore**.
- The **first post office in Kerala** was established at **Alappuzha in 1857**.
- He was the ruler when the **first coir factory in Kerala** was established at Alappuzha in **1859**.
- **Darragh Smail**, the first coir factory in Alappuzha, was established by:
  - **James Darragh**
  - **Henry Smail**
- **James Darragh** was an American of Irish descent.



# 13. AYILYAM THIRUNAL RAMA VARMA

## 1860–1880

### 32. What were the major reforms of Ayilyam Thirunal Rama Varma?

**Answer:**

- **Ayilyam Thirunal Rama Varma** ruled from **1860 to 1880**.
- He introduced the **Pandarapatta Proclamation in 1865**.
- The Pandarapatta Declaration is known as the **“Magna Carta of Travancore.”**
- The **Temple Entry Proclamation** is known as the **“Magna Carta of Kerala.”**
- He introduced the **Janmi-Kudiyan Valambaram in 1867**.
- He was the ruler during the confiscation of the newspaper **Sandishtavadi**.
- Travancore received the title **“Mathruka Rajya” or Model State** during his reign.
- He started **vernacular schools** in Travancore.
- He instituted a **special class for legal studies**.
- **Queen Victoria** bestowed the title of **“Maharaja”** on Ayilyam Thirunal in **1866**.



### 33. Who was T. Madhava Rao and what did he do during Ayilyam Thirunal's reign?

**Answer:**

- **T. Madhava Rao** was the famous Diwan of Ayilyam Thirunal.
- In **1860**, he started the **Public Works Department** in Travancore.
- In **1861**, the government opened the **Anchal (post office)** to the public.
- Important people in Ayilyam Thirunal's court included:
  - **Kerala Varma Valiya Koi Thampuran**
  - **A. R. Rajaraja Varma**
  - **Raja Ravi Varma**



### 34. What institutions and buildings were established during Ayilyam Thirunal's reign?

**Answer:**

Established during his reign:

- **Thiruvananthapuram District General Hospital**
- **Civil Hospital**
- **Mental Hospital**
- **Arts College**
- **Latex-related institution**
- **Kollam–Punalur Suspension Bridge**, 1877
- **Secretariat Building**, Thiruvananthapuram

Important facts:

- **Albert Henry** was the architect of the **Punalur Suspension Bridge**.
- **William Barton** was the architect of the **Secretariat Building**.
- The Secretariat Building was inaugurated in **1869**.
- **Swati Thirunal** worked hard to obtain the status of **Mathruka Rajyam (Model State)** for Travancore.
- **Ayilyam Thirunal** was the ruler when Travancore actually received the status of **Mathruka Rajyam**.



# 14. VISAKHAM THIRUNAL RAMA VARMA

## 1880–1885

### 35. What are the important facts about Visakham Thirunal?

**Answer:**

- **Visakham Thirunal Rama Varma** ruled from **1880 to 1885**.
- He was famous as a **scholar**.
- He was the ruler at the time of drafting the **Mullaperiyar Lease Agreement**.
- He described the Mullaperiyar lease as being **“written with my heart's blood.”**
- **Ramayankar** was his famous minister.
- A **complete land survey** was conducted in Travancore during his reign.
- He encouraged the cultivation of **tapioca** in Travancore.
- **Sree Visakh** is a variety of tapioca named in his memory.



### 36. What administrative and agricultural developments occurred during Visakham Thirunal's reign?

**Answer:**

- Visakham Thirunal separated **police and administration of justice** in Travancore.
- The **first cotton mill in Travancore** was established in **1881 in Kollam district**.
- Irrigation projects in **Nanchinadu** were expanded.
- **Nanchinad** is known as the **“Paddy field of Thiruvithamkur.”**



# 15. SREE MOOLAM THIRUNAL RAMA VARMA

## 1885–1924

### 37. What were the major social and political events during Sree Moolam Thirunal's reign?

**Answer:**

- **Sree Moolam Thirunal Rama Varma** ruled from **1885 to 1924**.
- He was the ruler to whom the:
  - **Malayali Memorial of 1891**
  - **First Ezhava Memorial of 1896**  
  were submitted.
- The **Malayali Memorial** was submitted on **1 January 1891**.
- **K. P. Shankara Menon** was the first person to sign it.
- **G. P. Pillai** was the second to sign it.
- **Dr. Palpu** was the third to sign it.
- The **First Ezhava Memorial** was submitted on **3 September 1896**.
- He passed the **Janmi-Kudiyan Regulation Act in 1896**.
- He made **primary education free**.
- He allowed children from **backward communities** to enter government schools.
- He started the **Archaeological Research Department**.
- He was the ruler when the **Vaikom Satyagraha** began on **30 March 1924**.



### 38. What are the important facts about representation and administration during Sree Moolam Thirunal's reign?

**Answer:**

- **Ayyankali** was the first Harijan to become a member of the **Sree Moolam Praja Sabha**.
- **P. Rajagopalachari** was the Diwan who exiled **Swadesabhimani Ramakrishna Pillai**.
- The **Travancore Legislative Council** came into force in **1888**.
- In **1904**, the Travancore Legislative Council became the **Sree Moolam Praja Sabha**.
- The first meeting of the Sree Moolam Praja Sabha was held in **1904 at VJT Hall**.



# 16. MULLAPERIYAR DAM

### 39. What are the important facts about the Mullaperiyar Lease Agreement and Dam?

**Answer:**

- **Visakham Thirunal** was the king when the **Mullaperiyar Lease Agreement** was written.
- **Sree Moolam Thirunal** was the king when the **Mullaperiyar Dam was inaugurated in 1895**.
- **Ramayankar** was the Diwan of Travancore who signed the **Periyar Lease Agreement** for the Mullaperiyar Dam.



### 40. Which important institutions were started during the era of Sree Moolam Thirunal?

**Answer:**

Important institutions and buildings started during his period included:

- **Finger Print Bureau**
- **Handwriting Library / Hastha Likhitha Library**
- **Women's College**
- **Law College**
- **Ayurveda College**
- **Sanskrit College**
- **Durguna Parihara Padasala**
- **Kanakakkunnu Palace**
- **Ayyankali Hall / VJT Hall**



# 17. PURADAM THIRUNAL SETHULAKSHMI BHAI

## 1924–1931

### 41. What were the major social reforms of Sethu Lakshmi Bhai?

**Answer:**

- **Puradam Thirunal Sethu Lakshmi Bhai** ruled from **1924 to 1931** as Regent.
- She abolished:
  - **Animal sacrifice**
  - **Devadasi system** in temples
- The Devadasi system was also called **Kudikari Sampradayam**.
- She was the ruler during:
  - **Suchindram Satyagraha**
  - **Tiruvarppu Satyagraha**
- A memorandum connected with the **Vaikom Satyagraha** was submitted to her.
- She electrified **Thiruvananthapuram in 1929**.
- She ruled as Regent because **Sri Chithira Thirunal was a minor**.
- **Gram Panchayats** were formed during her period.
- She launched a **free mid-day meal scheme**.
- She granted **freedom of movement to Avarnas through temple streets** in Travancore.
- She was the **only ruler of Travancore visited by Mahatma Gandhi**, in **1925**.
- She was the ruler when the **Nair Act** came into force.



### 42. What were the other important developments during Sethu Lakshmi Bhai's regency?

**Answer:**

- She ended **Marumakkathayam** in Travancore.
- The **Travancore Newspaper Act** was passed in **1926**.
- **Sree Moolam Thirunal** was the ruler when the **Vaikom Satyagraha began**.
- **Sethu Lakshmi Bhai** was the ruler when the **Vaikom Satyagraha ended**.



# 18. SRI CHITHIRA THIRUNAL BALARAMA VARMA

## 1931–1949

### 43. What are the major achievements and reforms of Sri Chithira Thirunal?

**Answer:**

- **Sri Chithira Thirunal Balarama Varma** ruled from **1931 to 1949**.
- He was the **last ruler of Travancore**.
- He issued the **Temple Entry Proclamation on 12 November 1936**.
- He ordered the **abolition of capital punishment** in Travancore.
- He was the **first King of Travancore to make a sea voyage**.
- He introduced **adult suffrage** in Travancore.
- He was the **first Chancellor of Travancore University**.
- He was the **first ruler of Kerala to visit the Pope**.
- He enacted the **Widow Remarriage Act in 1938**.
- He was the ruler during the **Punnapra-Vayalar Struggle in 1946**.
- He later held the position of **Rajapramukhan of Thirukochi**.
- He was the ruler of Travancore when **India became independent**.



### 44. What were the important political developments during Chithira Thirunal's reign?

**Answer:**

- **V. S. Subhamanya Iyer** was the chairman of the **Temple Entry Study Committee** appointed during Chithira Thirunal's reign.
- **Muhammad Habibullah** was the only Muslim Diwan of Travancore.
- The **Travancore State Congress** was formed in **1938**.
- **Pattom Thanu Pillai** led the formation of the Travancore State Congress.
- Chithira Thirunal died in **1991 at Kowdiar Palace**.
- His statue was installed at **Thiruvananthapuram University College**.



### 45. Who were the last Diwans of Travancore?

**Answer:**

- **C. P. Ramaswami Iyer** was the **last Diwan of Travancore**.
- **P. G. N. Unnithan** was the **last acting Diwan** of Travancore.
- **Muhammad Habibullah** was the **only Muslim Diwan** of Travancore.
